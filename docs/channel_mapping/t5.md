# T5 channel correspondence

Participant `braingate2/T5` appears in six datasets: two Human Communication datasets, [willet_2021](../../src/neuralshed/datasets/willet_2021/README.md) and [fan_2023](../../src/neuralshed/datasets/fan_2023/README.md), and four Human Motor datasets, [deo_2024](../../src/neuralshed/datasets/deo_2024/README.md), [wilson_2025](../../src/neuralshed/datasets/wilson_2025/README.md), [willsey_2025](../../src/neuralshed/datasets/willsey_2025/README.md) and [gusman_2025](../../src/neuralshed/datasets/gusman_2025/README.md). T5 has two 96-electrode Utah arrays in the hand knob of the left precentral gyrus. Every Study emits 192 columns per neural feature, one block of 96 per array:

| columns | willsey_2025 | all other datasets |
|---|---|---|
| 0-95 | `array_1` | `lateral_array` |
| 96-191 | `array_2` | `medial_array` |

The columns are in source order, except on wilson_2025 `t5.2017.08.04` and willsey_2025 `t5.2023.04.13`, where the Study swaps the two arrays back.

This page tests whether column `i` is the same electrode in all six. No recording is in two releases, but 17 days are: deo_2024 and wilson_2025 share seven, willet_2021 and wilson_2025 two, and fan_2023 shares eight with a 2022 cohort in wilson_2025's release (`raw_data/one_month_recal`) that its Study leaves out. On each shared day the two releases hold different blocks. gusman_2025 has one undated session.

| dataset | days | dates | neural feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| willet_2021 | 11 | 2019-05-08 to 2020-01-15 | crossings, -3.5 x RMS, 10 ms |
| fan_2023 | 26 | 2022-05-18 to 2023-10-09 | crossings, -4.5 x SD, 20 ms |
| deo_2024 | 13 | 2021-06-02 to 2021-10-13 | crossings, -3.5 x RMS, 20 ms |
| wilson_2025 | 103 | 2016-09-26 to 2021-07-26 | crossings, -3.5 x RMS, 20 ms, stored as Hz |
| wilson_2025 2022 cohort (not in the Study) | 11 | 2022-05-16 to 2022-07-06 | crossings, 20 ms |
| willsey_2025 | 7 | 2023-03-09 to 2023-04-13 | spike-band power, 50 ms |
| gusman_2025 | 1 | undated, blocks 21-23 | crossings and spike-band power, normalized, 20 ms |

## Conclusions

- willet_2021 and gusman_2025 state that columns 0-95 are the lateral array and 96-191 the medial array. No source states an electrode order.
- All six releases share one column order at the level of arrays and 32-channel banks, except willsey_2025 `t5.2023.04.13`, whose two arrays are swapped in all 20 blocks.
- The lateral and medial labels carry over to the other four datasets as a candidate.
- Electrode order within an array is a candidate. The data match under identity well beyond bank membership, but do not confirm each electrode.
- The willsey_2025 Study swaps columns 0-95 and 96-191 of `t5.2023.04.13` back, so the six Studies can be pooled by column index.

## Method

Each day is summarized by the channel correlation matrix at 100 ms, pooled over blocks after removing each block's mean and clipping each column at its 0.1st and 99.9th percentiles, and by each column's mean rate. Two days are compared under a hypothesis that maps the columns of one onto the other:

- Matrix score: Pearson correlation of the upper triangles. Hypotheses are all 720 orders of the six 32-channel front-end banks (identity and the array swap among them) and 200 random orders within each array.
- Rate score: Pearson correlation of `log(1 + mean)` per column (crossing datasets only).
- Bank-centred score: the matrix score after subtracting each bank-pair submatrix's off-diagonal mean, against 500 random orders within banks. This tests structure beyond bank membership.

Calibration:

- The two halves of each of 160 days rank identity first of 720 every time (median 0.81-0.90 by release).
- Consecutive days within a release rank identity first in 149 of 155 comparisons. The misses are low-information days, the willsey swap, and two wilson_2025 pairs where identity is second by 0.0012 or less.
- The documented wilson_2025 swap, taken as stored, scores 0.016 as stored and 0.341 swapped, and the swap is the best of 720.
- Across wilson_2025 days the identity score falls with the gap, from a median of 0.48 within 30 days to 0.16 beyond two years, while the swap stays at or below 0.14.

## Results

### Same day, different releases

| pair | days | identity first of 720 | matrix, identity | matrix, swap | rate, identity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| deo_2024 / wilson_2025 | 7 | 7 | 0.48-0.57 | 0.05-0.07 | 0.95-0.99 |
| willet_2021 / wilson_2025 | 2 | 2 | 0.47-0.51 | 0.06-0.10 | 0.92-0.93 |
| fan_2023 / wilson_2025 2022 cohort | 8 | 8 | 0.35-0.58 | at most 0.033 | 0.95-0.98 |

On the nine Study-to-Study days, the bank-centred score is 0.46-0.56 under identity against at most 0.10 for orders within banks. Some single exchanges of two electrodes score above identity, by at most 0.012, on every day and also between halves of the same day. These are sampling-level differences, not evidence for a reorder.

- Every loss of 0.03 or more involves a low-information day: wilson_2025 days in the first four months after implant compared over four years or more, wilson_2025 `t5.2017.05.31`, the fan_2023 days marked invalid (2023-02-28, 08-16, 10-09) or with three trials, and willsey_2025 03-21 and 03-23, which carry a signal shared across the medial array (mean within-array correlation 0.43 and 0.29, against 0.10-0.13 on other days).
- The most common winner over identity is an exchange of lateral banks 2 and 3 (39 of 148 losses), in 35 of them against willsey_2025 `t5.2023.03.09` or fan_2023 `t5.2022.05.18`. Against each release's median matrix, no day prefers it within its own release and none by more than 0.022, so it reflects those reference days and not a reorder.

gusman_2025, against each release's median matrix: identity ranks first of 720 for crossings (0.36-0.50, swap 0.05-0.09) and for spike-band power (0.34-0.67, swap 0.03-0.07). Its five LFP features are not tested.

### willsey_2025 `t5.2023.04.13`

- Against the median of the four willsey days without a shared signal, the 20 blocks score 0.02-0.12 as stored and 0.67-0.73 swapped. Blocks of those four days, each against the other three, score 0.66-0.83 as stored and at most 0.09 swapped.
- Against `t5.2023.04.06` the stored order ranks 386th of 720 and the swap first (0.737 against 0.069). The result holds without clipping and at 50 ms.
- Mean power is higher on columns 96-191 in every block of the other days and lower in every block of this day.
- The swap also wins against the nearest deo_2024, willet_2021 and wilson_2025 days (0.26-0.32 against 0.02-0.04).

No source mentions it. The Study swaps the two arrays back on this day.

### Within each release

- In all 1,054 blocks from days with two or more blocks, the block's column means match the rest of its day better as stored than swapped.
- Under willet_2021's `arrayGeometryMap` with column `k` as electrode `k + 1`, grid neighbours correlate more than other pairs, beyond what bank membership explains, in all willet_2021, deo_2024 and willsey_2025 days, 102 and 96 of 103 wilson_2025 days and 26 and 18 of 26 fan_2023 days (lateral and medial). This supports a shared electrode numbering, but cannot distinguish the grid from its rotations or reflections.

These tests share days, references and features, so they are not independent replications.

## Notes for use

- Pool T5 by column index across all six Studies. The willsey_2025 Study corrects `t5.2023.04.13` and the wilson_2025 Study `t5.2017.08.04`. Raw files of both releases need the swap separately.
- willsey_2025 `array_1` / `array_2` correspond to `lateral_array` / `medial_array` as a candidate.
- 17 days appear in two releases. A day-held-out split should keep each on one side across releases. gusman_2025 has no date to check.
- A decoder trained and tested day by day is unaffected by a consistent swap. A released decoder applied to corrected data needs its input weights reordered.
- Features differ in threshold (-3.5 x RMS, -4.5 x SD), bin width (10, 20, 50 ms), unit (counts, Hz), type (crossings, spike-band power) and normalization (gusman_2025). Corresponding electrodes across years do not imply the same neurons.
- The gusman_2025 README points to a channel-mapping table in [standardization.md](../standardization.md) that does not exist.
